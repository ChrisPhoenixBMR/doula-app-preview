Doula dashboard

Your command center: who's on-call, your family roster with due dates, and one-tap credential upload that becomes a verified badge.

Why it's built this way

Every screen maps to how doulas and families actually work together:

  • Discovery → search + the free meet & greet
  • Qualifications → verified credential badges (DONA, CAPPA, CPR, lactation)
  • Prenatal visits → scheduling + journey tracker
  • On-call period → status banner + 24/7 messaging
  • Postpartum → resources + follow-up
  • Holistic care → first-class offerings, not afterthoughts

Built to honor that doulas spend ~76 hours with each family — this app is the connective tissue for that whole relationship.

The thinking

Grounded in how doulas and their clients really work together.

For our Afro-Latina queens too 🌺

Bilingüe · Inclusive

Lauren's right — Latinas are Black too. Hispanic-heritage & Afro-Latina mamas need this just as much. So the app can speak English & Español from day one, the warm-brown palette already embraces every shade of our queens, and the name itself can carry both heritages. Nothing above changes — these are more options layered on top of the work you already loved.
